Dashboard background photographs
Generated for OBDLink Diag v0.5.2.

These JPEG files were generated for this app. They are not Ford Motor Company
marketing photos, screenshots, or files copied from the web. They are original
generated pictures of a late-model full-size pickup cabin, including a physical
analog instrument cluster and a digital instrument cluster. They ship with this
project under the MIT license (see LICENSE).

No Ford oval or Ford wordmark was left in the files. A readable embossed word
on the steering wheel of digital-dash.jpg was painted out with nearby leather
before the file was saved.

Each file is a 1920x1080 baseline JPEG, so a 1080p dashboard can show it
without upscaling. The app draws each photo once with cover stretch
(UniformToFill) and a light dark scrim. The photos are not tiled.
